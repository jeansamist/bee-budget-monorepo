## Features

Heading: Everything your money does, *in one place.*

1. **Every wallet, one balance.** Orange Money, MTN MoMo, the cash in
   your pocket, your bank account. Each wallet keeps its own balance and
   trend, and BeeBudget adds them up for you.
2. **Incomes and expenses, sorted.** A salary, a gift, a freelance
   payment. Groceries, transport, bills. Categories come ready to use, and
   you can add your own.
3. **Move money, fees included.** Cashing out MoMo or topping up a
   wallet? Record the transfer once, fee and all, and both balances stay
   right.
4. **The week, the month, the year.** Spending by category, income
   against expenses, and how each wallet evolves over time.
5. **Know who you pay, and who pays you.** Link people and businesses to
   your transactions and see what you have exchanged with each of them.
6. **Light or dark, your call.** BeeBudget follows your system theme.
   Press D to switch at any time.

## How it works

Heading: Up and running *in three steps.*

1. **Create your account.** Sign up with your email. Your wallets and
   categories are set up for you.
2. **Log as you go.** Add incomes, expenses and transfers in a few taps,
   from your phone or your computer.
3. **Read the story.** Your dashboard and analytics show where your money
   comes from and where it goes.

## FAQ

Heading: Questions, *answered.*

- **Does BeeBudget connect to my Orange Money or MTN MoMo account?**
  No. BeeBudget never asks for your Mobile Money or bank credentials. You
  record your transactions yourself, so you decide what is tracked.
- **Which currency does BeeBudget use?**
  Amounts are in CFA francs (XAF).
- **Can I track transfer and withdrawal fees?**
  Yes. Expenses and transfers between wallets both have a fee field, so
  your balances match what is really left.
- **Can I use it on my phone?**
  Yes. BeeBudget runs in your browser and adapts to any screen size.
- **Do I have to set everything up myself?**
  No. When you sign up, BeeBudget creates Orange Money, MTN MoMo and Cash
  wallets and common categories. Rename, add or remove them whenever you
  like.
